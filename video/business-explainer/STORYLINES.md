# Storylines: the business film

The one thing: **let your users build the product they need, safely, and sell a platform they finish themselves.** For a business owner or CEO of a software product, not a product manager: the pain is the queue and the churn, the turn is a prompt box in the product, the five steps are the answer to "is that safe?", the close is one sentence. Same visual language as "How ACRYL works" (grid paper, Manrope and DM Mono, spectrum on plugins and one caption word, the app window with its chat column), silent and loopable for the site, about 60 s.

## A. The prompt box (recommended: a stranger follows one customer's request from the queue to the live change)

- World and device: one customer, one small request ("Add overdue invoices to my dashboard"), worked through to the end. The same app window all film.
- First two seconds: the customer's app, a "Request a feature" form already typing the sentence.
- Beats: request, it joins the queue, months pass, the customer builds a copy of the product themselves, then the same request typed into a prompt box inside the product and live for the customer and their team while another company's app is unchanged, then the five steps as five small diagrams that answer "why is that safe", then the closing line and acryl.dev.
- Look: the first film's: grid paper, ink, spectrum only on plugins and one word per caption. Sound: none (it plays muted in the hero).

## B. Two product teams (split screen to one)

- World and device: a split frame, left the product team with a roadmap meeting and a queue, right the same customer with a prompt box; the left side collapses away once the right side has shipped the change.
- First two seconds: both halves at once, the same request on each.
- Beats: split, the left waits, the right is live, the left folds, the five steps, the line.
- Bolder, but the five steps have no room and the viewer must compare two screens at once.

## C. The layer cake (the object is the explanation)

- World and device: one stack of bands (foundation, your layer, their layer) that grows as the film goes; every claim is a band.
- First two seconds: a single band, "FOUNDATION", with a customer's request landing on it and bouncing off.
- Beats: request bounces, the stack grows a layer per step, a release slides in underneath and the layers above stay, the line.
- Clear for the five steps, weak on the pain: no person, no queue, no churn.

Picked: A, the one that opens on a person's need and keeps one example through every step.
