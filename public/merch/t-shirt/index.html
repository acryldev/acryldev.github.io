T-shirt

Two designs, each for a black shirt with white ink and a white shirt with black ink. Print files are vector SVG and 300 dpi PNG with a transparent background, sized for a 15 inch wide print area.

Logo and QR

The ACRYL logo across the chest and a QR code to acryl.dev below it, drawn as rounded dots in the shirt's single ink with nothing behind it. White dots on the black shirt make an inverted code, which phone cameras read.
